# Remixing ralphd

ralphd is open source under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE) and is **closed to pull requests, open to remix**
(see [a new model for open source](https://ghuntley.com/remix/)). You may use, change and
redistribute it freely; the maintainer just doesn't take patches.

## How to remix

1. **Fork it, or copy it.** The licence already allows it; no permission is needed.
   Keep the `LICENSE` and your copyright notices, and mark your changes.
2. **Describe the change as an idea.** One or two sentences in your fork's README: what
   behaviour or architecture is different, and why. Ideas are the useful unit; patches are not.
3. **Let an agent do the porting.** Point your coding agent at `SPEC.md` and this repository,
   say what you want, and keep the result in your fork. Re-sync from upstream when you like.
4. **Tell me, optionally.** Open a thread in [Discussions](https://github.com/n-orlov/ralphd/discussions)
   with a link to your fork. The GitHub network graph also lists remixes.

## What to expect from upstream

- Pull requests from outside are not accepted and are not reviewed.
- Issues and Discussions are open for ideas, bug reports and links to remixes.
  They are read when time allows. There is no response-time promise and no maintenance duty.
- Good ideas may be reimplemented upstream, or ignored. Credit is given where I know the source.
- Security problems: use the repository's private security advisory form on GitHub.
